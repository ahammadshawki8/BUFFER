# Media sources: interviews, news and stock footage

Found by web search on 9 October 2026. Titles and descriptions come from search results; **watch each one before
using it**, check the date and place, and note the exact timestamps you want.

## Using other people's footage in the submission

- **YouTube, news and NGO videos are copyrighted.** Use them as research and background for the team, or show a
  short clip only with permission from the owner (NGOs such as WaterAid and UNDP often agree for non-commercial,
  educational use if asked). Always credit on screen: "Footage: WaterAid Bangladesh" and list it in the disclosure.
- **Pixabay and Pexels clips** are free to use without permission under their licences (no attribution required,
  though crediting is good practice). These are the safe choice for b-roll.
- Never present someone else's interview as a conversation the team held. The team's own three conversations are
  the stakeholder evidence; these videos only add context.
